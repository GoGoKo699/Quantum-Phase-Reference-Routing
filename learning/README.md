# Learning route

Follow one square-lattice bond built from five two-hop paths. Each path joins Alice to Bob through its own intermediate laboratory. The story begins with one excitation on one elementary link and ends with a lattice of heralded Bell pairs.

- [Reference-frame bridge](BRS_BRIDGE.md) connects Bartlett, Rudolph and Spekkens's review to this network.
- [M01–M12](MILESTONES.md#phase-i--background-and-preliminaries) build up the link, the phase model, the available operations and the routing task.
- [M13–M30](MILESTONES.md#phase-ii--mechanism-and-innovation) assemble the routing protocol, prove its parallel-path success law and explore its scale and limits. [M23](MILESTONES.md#m23-shared-components-and-the-wider-graph-question) interprets sector eligibility on a random four-regular graph, and [M24](MILESTONES.md#m24-why-a-bridge-still-blocks-routing) distinguishes individual routing from color-coalition entanglement.
- [Scientific account](../science/README.md) provides the formal statements and proofs.

The 12 preliminaries establish the ingredients needed to read a charge record. The 18 narrative milestones give the mechanism room to develop from one active path to a usable network. Each milestone pairs its common understanding target with one practice question and expandable feedback.
